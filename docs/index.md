# AI Glossary

Plain-English explanations of AI terms, concepts, and technologies — written for everyone, not just engineers.

## Why This Exists

AI terminology moves fast and often assumes you already know what the terms mean. This glossary fills that gap with clear, accessible explanations that anyone can understand — whether you're a curious reader, a business professional, or someone just starting to explore AI.

## How to Use It

- **Browse by tier** — start with Tier 1 if you're new to AI, work your way up
- **Follow the links** — each entry connects to related terms for deeper understanding
- **Share it** — when someone asks "what does that mean?" point them here instead of explaining from scratch
